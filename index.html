Privacy Policy — MELONPOT

Last updated: 25 October 2026

MELONPOT (“the game”, package com.r4ptor.melonpot) is published by RaptyPlay. This policy explains how the game handles your information. Short version: it doesn’t collect anything.

Data we collect

None. MELONPOT does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or usage data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no tracking.

Internet use

The game uses the internet permission for one thing only: when it starts, it downloads a small public announcement file (news.json) from GitHub Pages to check whether an update is available and to show messages from the developer in the in-game mailbox. No information about you or your game is sent in this request.

As with any internet connection, the server that hosts the file (GitHub) can see your device’s IP address. That is handled under GitHub’s privacy statement; RaptyPlay does not receive or store it.

If you are offline, the game simply skips this check and works fully without internet. The “Update” button opens Google Play, where Google’s own privacy policy applies.

Data stored on your device

The game saves the following only on your device, never uploaded anywhere:

  • Settings (language, music/SFX volume, control sensitivity).
  • Local run history and high scores.

This data never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the game removes it.

Sharing a score card

When you tap Share, the game creates an image of your score and hands it to the Android share sheet (and saves a copy to your gallery). What happens next is controlled by the app you choose to share to (e.g. WhatsApp) and that app’s own privacy policy — MELONPOT itself sends nothing.

Ads and analytics

The game currently contains no advertising and no analytics SDKs. If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the store listing’s Data Safety section will be updated accordingly.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
